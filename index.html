⚠️ Prohibited Brand Applications

  • Never alter, stretch, skew, or rotate the crest aspect ratio.
  • Never substitute Signature Gold with standard yellow or digital gradients.
  • Never overlay the mark on low-contrast patterned photography without a solid matte backing.

Typography Hierarchy

Typeface Standards

Our typography pairs high-end luxury serif displays with ultra-clean modern sans-serif interface text.

Primary Display Serif
Cinzel Display

Utilized exclusively for primary titles, luxury banners, section headers, and product naming badges.

Body & UI Sans-Serif
Plus Jakarta Sans

Utilized for paragraphs, navigation menus, button labels, and high-density technical data.

Scale & Leading Rule

Headings implement strict tracking with Cinzel Bold. Body text requires Plus Jakarta Sans Regular with a minimum 1.5 line height ratio.

Visual Direction

Art Direction & Imagery

All photographic and digital imagery must convey natural warmth, tactile luxury, and structural simplicity.

Lighting & Atmosphere

Soft, warm natural lighting mimicking golden hour sunlight. Avoid cold, direct studio flash photography.

Background Materials

Utilize natural silk fabrics (#F4EAD8), matte micro-cement textures, or dark burgundy velvet surfaces.

Framing Standard

Maintain a strict minimum of 20% clear negative space surrounding fragrance bottles in all digital ad placements.

Layout Architecture

Spacing & Grid Layouts

Macro Spacing (32px - 48px)
