Typography Hierarchy

Typeface Standards

Our typography strategy pairs high-end luxury serif displays with ultra-clean, readable modern sans-serif interface text.

Primary Display Serif
Cinzel Display

Utilized exclusively for primary titles, luxury banners, section headers, and product naming badges.

Body & UI Sans-Serif
Plus Jakarta Sans

Utilized for paragraphs, navigation menus, button labels, and high-density technical data structures.

Scale & Leading Rule

Headings implement strict letter-tracking with Cinzel Bold. Body text requires Plus Jakarta Sans Regular with a minimum 1.5 line height ratio.

◆ Executive Directive & Discussion Notes
"Typeface choices define brand texture. Cinzel conveys timeless elegance, whereas Plus Jakarta Sans ensures absolute legibility on modern high-density screens."
Editorial Copywriting

Tone of Voice

Velour Essence communicates with quiet authority, absolute clarity, product transparency, and refined warmth.

DO
"Master-blended fine fragrances crafted with rare botanical notes and delivered directly to your doorstep."
DON'T
"Buy cheap designer knockoffs now before our limited stock runs out!!"
DO
"Every order includes a complimentary 2ml sample vial so you can experience the scent before unsealing."
DON'T
"No refunds, returns, or exchanges allowed under any circumstances whatsoever."
◆ Executive Directive & Discussion Notes
"Our copywriting avoids aggressive marketing hype or artificial urgency. We speak to our clientele as discerning connoisseurs who value sophisticated curation."
Visual Direction

Art Direction & Imagery

All photographic and digital imagery must convey natural warmth, tactile luxury, and structural simplicity.

Lighting & Atmosphere

Soft, warm natural lighting mimicking golden hour sunlight. Avoid cold, direct studio flash photography.

Background Materials

Utilize natural silk fabrics (#F4EAD8), matte micro-cement textures, or dark burgundy velvet surfaces.

Framing Standard

Maintain a strict minimum of 20% clear negative space surrounding fragrance bottles in all digital ad placements.

◆ Executive Directive & Discussion Notes
"Photography should feel tactile and breathe naturally. Over-saturated digital processing or cluttered background elements compromise the brand's perception of premium exclusivity."
Layout Architecture

Spacing & Grid Layouts

The layout system operates on a flexible 8pt scaling rhythm to maintain vertical and horizontal harmony.

Micro Spacing (4px - 8px)

Used for tight component groupings, badge padding, and icon margins.

Base Spacing (16px - 24px)

Standard padding for content cards, form inputs, and grid gaps.

Macro Spacing (32px - 48px)

Section dividers, viewport page padding, and major layout blocks.

◆ Executive Directive & Discussion Notes
"Strict adherence to grid alignment and tokenized spacing prevents visual clutter, allowing users to navigate content seamlessly across both mobile and desktop viewports."
