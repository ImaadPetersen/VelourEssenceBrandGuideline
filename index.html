Emblem System

Logo & Crest Standards

Our emblem fuses classical heraldry with modern luxury refinement. Always preserve the surrounding protective clear zone.

Primary Crest
Optimized for light cream and white backgrounds
Inverted Crest
Mandatory for deep Velour Burgundy surfaces

⚠️ Prohibited Brand Applications

  • Never distort, skew, stretch, or alter the geometric proportions of the emblem.
  • Never substitute unauthorized colors or replace Signature Gold with unapproved metallic tones.
  • Never overlay the crest on low-contrast photo backgrounds without a solid backing shield.
  • Never apply unapproved visual filters such as drop shadows, glows, bevels, or heavy gradients.

Typographic Hierarchy

Typeface Standards

Our dual-font typographic architecture balances editorial luxury with digital clarity.

Primary Display / Editorial
Cinzel Display

Reserved for section titles, wordmarks, product names, and headline statements. Conveys classical authority and luxury refinement.

UI & Body Copy
Plus Jakarta Sans

Applied to body paragraphs, button labels, navigation menus, and form fields. Guarantees modern legibility across all screen viewports.

Hierarchy Guidance

Titles use Cinzel Bold with +0.5px letter spacing. Paragraph body text uses Plus Jakarta Sans Regular with a 1.5 line height ratio.

Communication Standards

Tone & Editorial Voice

Velour Essence communicates with quiet authority, warmth, and transparent luxury.

DO
"Master-blended fine fragrances inspired by legendary notes, crafted and delivered without traditional retail markups."
DON'T
"Buy our super cheap designer knockoffs right now before they sell out!!"
DO
"Every bottle includes a risk-free 2ml test sample so you can verify your signature scent before opening."
DON'T
"No refunds, returns, or exchanges under any circumstances after purchase."
Visual Identity

Art Direction & Imagery

Visual collateral must always evoke warmth, tactile texture, and minimalist luxury.

Lighting & Atmosphere

Utilize soft, diffused lighting mimicking natural morning sunlight or warm golden hour tones. Avoid harsh artificial flashes or saturated color casts.

Background & Composition

Incorporate clean architectural surfaces, neutral cream gradients, or rich silk textures (#F4EAD8). Maintain generous white space around all product shots.

Product Presentation Rule

Keep flacons centered with a minimum of 20% clear margin surrounding the item in all social and print crops.
