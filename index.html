⚠️ Prohibited Brand Applications

  • Never alter, stretch, or skew the crest aspect ratio.
  • Never substitute Signature Gold with neon or standard primary yellow.
  • Never overlay the crest on busy or low-contrast photographic patterns.

Typography Hierarchy

Typeface Standards

Our typography pairs high-end luxury serif displays with ultra-clean modern sans-serif body type.

Primary Display Serif
Cinzel Display

Utilized for primary titles, luxury banners, section titles, and product logos.

Body & UI Sans-Serif
Plus Jakarta Sans

Utilized for paragraphs, nav menus, buttons, and high-readability body copy.

Hierarchy Guidelines

Headings use Cinzel Bold. Paragraphs use Plus Jakarta Sans Regular with 1.5 line height for maximum legibility.

Brand Voice

Tone of Voice

Velour Essence communicates with quiet authority, clarity, and refined warmth.

DO
"Master-blended fine fragrances crafted with rare notes and delivered directly to your doorstep."
DON'T
"Buy cheap designer knockoffs now before our limited stock runs out!!"
DO
"Every order includes a complimentary 2ml sample vial so you can experience the scent before unsealing."
DON'T
"No refunds, returns, or exchanges allowed under any circumstances."
Visual Direction

Art Direction & Imagery

All imagery must convey natural warmth, tactile luxury, and simple elegance.

Lighting & Atmosphere

Soft, warm natural lighting mimicking golden hour sunlight. Avoid cold, direct flash photography.

Background Materials

Utilize natural silk fabrics (#F4EAD8), matte micro-cement, or dark burgundy velvet surfaces.

Framing Standard

Maintain a minimum of 20% clear space surrounding fragrance bottles in all marketing materials.
